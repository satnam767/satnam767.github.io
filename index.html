About Me

About Satnam Singh

I am a graduate in Mathematics (Honours) who dedicated 20 years of service to the Indian Air Force as a Flight Gunner. My career spanned diverse posting locations across India, providing extensive exposure to varied operational environments and demanding conditions.

During my service, I gained hands-on training and operational experience with Mi-17, Mi-17 V5, and IV Helicopters. I had the privilege of serving as a Gunner Instructor at Yelahanka, where I trained and mentored personnel, sharing operational knowledge and upholding professional standards.

I also served 3 years at BRD, contributing to the broader organisational mission. My pursuit of learning led me to complete a Drone/Quadcopter course from IIT Ropar, bridging my aviation background with emerging technology.

Today, I am actively exploring Artificial Intelligence, Internet of Things (IoT), and Cybersecurity — channeling the discipline, precision, and adaptability cultivated over two decades of military service into the technology domain.
